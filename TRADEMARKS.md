# Ryzova project names and marks — use policy

The project's preferred public names are **Ryzova Kernel** (kernel project), **RyzovaOS** (future official OS), and **RyzovaTech** (project organization). The repository slug `Ryzova-Kernal` is retained for existing links; **Kernel** is the product spelling.

**Code licenses do not automatically grant rights to project names, logos or branding.** This policy applies only to marks the project owner actually owns or is otherwise authorized to govern. No registered-trademark status is claimed here.

## Generally permitted

- Accurate factual references (e.g., “based on Ryzova Kernel”) and fair attribution.
- Linking to the official repository and identifying a version, without implying sponsorship.
- Community tutorials, compatibility notes and educational use that is not misleading.

## Request permission before

- Naming a derivative operating system “RyzovaOS,” “Official Ryzova Kernel,” or other confusingly official variants.
- Using the project logo as the principal branding of a different product.
- Claiming Ryzova sponsorship, security certification, partnership, or paid support.
- Offering third-party merchandise with official-looking Ryzova marks.

Third-party forks are welcome under the applicable **code licenses**. When presenting independently maintained variants, choose a distinct name and clearly acknowledge the upstream source. Third-party 9front and Plan 9 identities likewise must not be used in ways that imply endorsement.

For proposed commercial brand use, obtain **written permission from the actual rights holder**. This file is a project policy proposal, not evidence of registration or ownership.
