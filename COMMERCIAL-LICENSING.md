# Ryzova Kernel — GPLv3 and commercial-use clarification

**Current policy:** Ryzova does not offer a separate proprietary/commercial relicensing program for Ryzova Kernel project code. Its stated outbound policy for newly authored, rights-controlled Ryzova source is **GPL-3.0-only**; inherited and third-party files retain their actual individual licenses.

## Compliant commercial use is permitted

The GNU GPL version 3 does **not** forbid commercial use, paid distribution, consulting, integration or support. A commercial organization needs no paid Ryzova license merely to use or redistribute code under the terms of its applicable GPLv3 license. Distribution and corresponding-source obligations still apply when required by the relevant license.

This project does not announce a proprietary-license fee, dual-license sales program or special exception to the GPL. Support services and engineering contracts, if ever offered, are separate from permission to use GPL-covered code.

## Legacy AGPLv3 and contributor rights

Past copies offered under AGPL-3.0-only retain those valid grants. The current GPLv3 policy is **not** a blanket relicensing of every historical file: a specific AGPL-marked file changes its current outbound offer only where necessary rights are verified and the file is deliberately updated.

A normal pull request does not assign copyright to Ryzova. Neither Ryzova nor a contributor may purport to license code that is owned by someone else without adequate rights. Existing agreements, if any, require their own review; this policy does not purport to cancel third-party contractual rights.

## Unchanged inherited and third-party terms

Original Plan 9/9front notices, upstream history, and copyright statements remain in force; bundled drivers, programs, fonts and assets may have separate restrictions. There is **no project-wide representation that all files can be redistributed under GPLv3 alone**, nor any implicit trademark, patent, warranty, indemnity, or release certification grant.

See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md), [contributor licensing](docs/CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSING.md), [GPLv3 migration audit](docs/GPLV3-MIGRATION-AUDIT.md), [upstream notice](lib/legal/NOTICE), and [trademarks](TRADEMARKS.md).

*This document states an open-source policy, not legal clearance of a combined kernel image or third-party assets.*
